# The man behind the words: share card, audio, fact check

**Series:** The man behind the words / మాట వెనుక మనిషి

**Title:** The man behind the words
**Dek:** Why 1 November is Andhra Pradesh's birthday, and who paid for it.
**Pull quote (share card):** "He gave his breath so that ours could carry these words." (11 words)
**Telugu pull quote:** "మన ఊపిరి ఈ మాటల్ని మోయడానికి, ఆయన తన ఊపిరిని వదిలేశారు."

## 60-second audio script (for commuters)

> Say one word in Telugu. *Amma.* Easy, isn't it?
>
> Seventy-four years ago, it wasn't. Telugu people were split between two states, and in both, the government spoke another language.
>
> Potti Sriramulu was a railway engineer who had lost his wife and child. He left everything and followed Gandhi.
>
> On 19 October 1952, in a borrowed house in Mylapore, Madras, he stopped eating. He wanted one thing: a state for the Telugu people.
>
> Day ten. Day thirty. Day fifty. He did not give in.
>
> On the fifty-eighth day, 15 December 1952, he died. He was fifty-one.
>
> Four days later, Nehru announced Andhra State. And on 1 November 1956, Andhra Pradesh was born.
>
> So the next time you say *Amma*, remember: he gave his breath so that ours could carry these words.
>
> *మాట మనది. మనిషి ఆయన.* The words are ours. The man was him.

(About 150 words: one minute at a calm speaking pace.)
