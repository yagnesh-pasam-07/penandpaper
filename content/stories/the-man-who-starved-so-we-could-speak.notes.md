## Facts used (checked)
Born 16 March 1901, Madras; sanitary engineering, Great Indian Peninsula Railway; wife and child died 1928 (aged 27); left the railway in 1930 after his mother's death; Sabarmati, Salt Satyagraha, jail; Nellore temple-entry fast; Gandhi's "eleven more followers" (attributed); fast from 19 October 1952 at Bulusu Sambamurthy's house, 126 Royapettah High Road, Mylapore; died 15 December 1952, day 58, aged 51; police firing in Anakapalle and Vijayawada, seven killed; Nehru's announcement 19 December 1952; Andhra State 1 October 1953 (Kurnool); Andhra Pradesh 1 November 1956; Nellore renamed Sri Potti Sriramulu Nellore in 2008.
