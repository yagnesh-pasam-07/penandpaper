# Pen & Paper — Visual Team Task Planner

A simple, visual task planner for small teams (2–20 people).

Team leads dump their thoughts onto a canvas, turn them into estimated tasks, and drag them onto team members, while the tool tracks each person's allocated hours against their available capacity.

**Think → Estimate → Assign.**

## How it works

1. **Brain dump**: capture ideas as floating cards on a canvas.
2. **Turn thoughts into tasks**: add a title, description, and hour estimate.
3. **Set team capacity**: define each member's available hours for the day.
4. **Drag and drop**: assign tasks to members and see `allocated / available` hours update live.
5. **Review and finalize**: spot overloaded or underutilized members, rebalance, and finalize the plan.
